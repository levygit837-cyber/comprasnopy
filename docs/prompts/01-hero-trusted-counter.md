# Hero image prompt: Trusted pharmacy counter

## Purpose
Replace the first gradient hero slide with a designed editorial pharmacy image that creates immediate trust. This is slide 1 of 4. The hero block should use four real image slides, not two colored gradient slides.

## Visual identity lock
- Brand world: warm ivory pharmacy, deep herbal green authority, restrained amber detail.
- Palette: warm ivory `#fbfbf9`, primary herbal green `#536e2c`, deep forest `#182110`, sage `#e5ead2`, accent amber `#b0822a`.
- Image style: premium product-photography base with Photoshop-like motion graphics, translucent acrylic, soft botanical shadows, clean negative space.
- No text, logos, labels, brand names, UI, or readable packaging inside the image.

## Image prompt
Create a high-end designed editorial pharmacy counter scene for a premium neighborhood pharmacy marketplace. Warm ivory seamless background, pale oak counter, sealed medicine and wellness product boxes with blank labels, amber glass bottle, folded prescription-like paper with no writing, a clean green acrylic tray, a subtle tamper-evident seal shape with no text, and one pharmacist hand in a white coat arranging the order with calm precision. Add translucent herbal-green ribbon shapes and faint motion-graphic contour lines around the products, as if composed in Photoshop for a modern pharmacy campaign. Lighting is soft top-left studio light with gentle botanical leaf shadows, warm ivory highlights, deep forest-green depth on the right, restrained amber accent reflections. Composition must feel trustworthy, professional, curated, human, and premium, not like stock photography. Leave the left 42 percent of the image clean and darker with a soft green gradient for UI text overlay. Product cluster sits on the right, crisp, tactile, and organized. Ultra realistic materials, editorial art direction, shallow depth of field, refined color grade, no readable text.

## Negative prompt
Readable text, logos, brand names, fake labels, busy shelves, hospital bed, patient, doctor face, syringes in use, injection scene, clutter, neon colors, blue clinical tech look, cheap stock photo, generic smiley pharmacist, AI artifacts, warped hands, extra fingers, blurry products, over-sharpening, plastic-looking packaging, exaggerated glow, text baked into image.

## Composition notes
- Text-safe zone: left 42 percent, darkened by a soft forest-green overlay in the UI layer.
- Subject zone: right 58 percent, product arrangement angled from lower-right to center.
- Trust cues: sealed packaging, clean hands, blank prescription paper, curated tray, controlled lighting.
- Keep the image designed and editorial, not documentary. It should feel like a premium campaign still.

## Interaction and motion notes
- Use a 4-segment progress bar, each segment fills over 6 seconds and resets on manual navigation.
- Pause autoplay on hover, focus, drag, or touch hold.
- Add invisible tap zones: left third goes previous, right third goes next.
- Desktop hover: image drifts 1.5 percent in the opposite direction of cursor movement, products stay subtle.
- Mobile: show a small partial peek of the next slide edge for the first 2 seconds to teach swipe.
- Micro-test: segmented progress under copy vs bottom-center progress. Measure next-slide taps, CTA clicks, and swipe completion.

## Technical specs
- Aspect ratio: 1400 x 460 display, generate at 2800 x 920 for retina.
- Safe crop: keep key products inside the central-right 55 percent so mobile crops still work.
- Format: AVIF or WebP export, high quality, target below 450 KB after compression.
- Accessibility: image is decorative when overlaid with text, alt can summarize the concept if rendered as an image element.
